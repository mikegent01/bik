# Bowser's Castle, 1035 BF — Foundry VTT actors (Session III: the castle comes down)

Twenty-seven dnd5e NPC exports (system 5.3.3, core 14) for the third session
of the Bowser's Castle coup chain — the night of 18 Harvestide, 1035 BF,
picking up where `the_assault_on_bowsers_castle` leaves off: the throne-room
wall is open, the dragon is on the building, the garrison is going out through
the lava tunnel, and Bowser is fighting his way **down** his own castle. The
GM run-sheet is
[`docs/sessions/bowsers-castle-1035-the-castle-comes-down.md`](../../../docs/sessions/bowsers-castle-1035-the-castle-comes-down.md).

Two sides and a sideplot, filed as three Foundry folders under *Bowser's
Castle 1035 BF*:

- **Side A — Bowser's Line** (friendly) — the loyal garrison and **Omega
  Bowser**, the form Bowser takes if the castle lands on him.
- **Side B — Fawthful's Forces** (hostile) — Fawthful, Mimbus, Cackletta, the
  Jester, the supplied dragon, the drone, the traitors, Fawthful's own troops,
  and the Switch Hag (neutral; she attacks nobody).
- **The Remnant at the Track** — Koopa Killer Killa and the Goomba who took
  his chances, for the Toad Town Station thread the GM runs later.

All twenty-seven are generated by
[`tools/build-bowsers-castle-1035-actors.py`](../../../tools/build-bowsers-castle-1035-actors.py)
— **do not hand-edit**; change the generator and re-run it (`check-all` runs
its `--check`). The generator imports the 955 packet's item factories, ability
blocks and icon shelf, so the two era packets cannot drift apart.

## Install

1. Tokens: copy `Reputation-Matrix2/portraits/bowsers-castle-1035/` to
   `<Foundry Data>/portraits/bowsers-castle-1035/`. The generic Koopa Troop
   troops reuse `portraits/peachs-castle-955/foe-*.png`, and the four named
   era copies reuse the library portraits (`portraits/fawful_v2.png`,
   `goomba_executive.png`, `jester_goomba.webp`,
   `player/fullbody/cackletta.png`), so install those too — or let the bridge
   do all of it:
   `python3 tools/foundry-bridge.py install-images Reputation-Matrix2/actors/bowsers-castle-1035 --foundry-data "<Foundry Data>"`.
   With the launcher's art URL set in the module (1.8+), nothing needs copying.
2. **One click:** with the
   [Waluipedia Mass Import module](../../Foundry/mass_import/README.md)
   enabled, Actors sidebar → **Import** → pick *Bowser's Castle 1035 BF* from
   the list (URL
   `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mikegent01/bik/gh-pages/Reputation-Matrix2/actors/bowsers-castle-1035/import.json`),
   or upload [`import.json`](import.json), or paste
   [`macros/import-bowsers-castle-1035.js`](../../Foundry/mass_import/macros/import-bowsers-castle-1035.js)
   into a script macro. It creates the three folders, all twenty-seven actors
   with their fixed `_id`s, and reports any token the server cannot find.
   Running it again after a rebuild **updates** them in place. The sheets
   suite also folds this packet into the world's everything-import, exactly
   as it does the 955 packet (the folder scheme's `eras` names both).
3. Prototype tokens are unlinked, so six Loyal Koopas are six HP pools. Omega
   Bowser is a 4×4 token; the dragon 3×3.

`import.json` is generated —
`python3 tools/foundry-bridge.py combine Reputation-Matrix2/actors/bowsers-castle-1035 --out Reputation-Matrix2/actors/bowsers-castle-1035/import.json --world bowsers-castle-1035`
— and `check-all` fails when it drifts from the files.

## Omega Bowser — the swap

The present-day Bowser is a player character (level 8 in the ledger) and is
**not** built here. `Omega Bowser (1035 BF)` is his second form for one
night:

- **Trigger.** Bowser would drop to 0 hit points from bludgeoning damage dealt
  by a collapsing floor or wall, a falling tower, or the crush of a Huge or
  larger creature — the dragon's *Wall Breaker*, the Switch Hag's countdown,
  a dropped gallery. The GM may call the Omega instead of unconsciousness.
- **Swap.** Right-click Bowser's token → *Transform* and drop this sheet on
  him (keep mental scores), or swap the token for the 4×4 Omega token and run
  this sheet beside his. Omega starts at full hit points in the space where
  Bowser was buried.
- **Clock.** Ten rounds, or until Omega drops to 0. Bowser comes back prone
  in the nearest free space with 1 hit point + his Constitution modifier per
  round the Omega lasted, and one level of exhaustion.

CR 14 on purpose: for ten rounds he outclasses the dragon and Fawthful
together. He is not a tool for the rest of the campaign.

## Provenance

Every number is on the sheet's biography with the record it comes from
(events.json `bowser_throne_room_compromise`, `the_assault_on_bowsers_castle`;
the `fawthful`, `mimbus`, `cackletta`, `thejestergoomba` and `bowser`
articles). Names the record does not give — the dragon, the hag, the Goomba —
are filed by role, as the archive files the Jester and the Koopa Prisoner. The
nine named tokens are generated art cut on magenta through
`tools/make-token-plates.py` (`background_audit()` clean); the four Fawthful
generics are the Beanbean 3D packet's cutouts re-padded to 512 px.
