# The Liberated Toads cohorts — Foundry VTT actors

Eighty-nine dnd5e NPC exports (system 5.3.3, core 14) for the Liberated Toads
of the *Pond Patrol docket* — the cohort rosters on the
[`#/pond-docket`](../../../index.html#/pond-docket) page, as of Highsun 24,
1040 BF. The seventy-five Toadslist toads with articles of their own already
have sheets in the cast packet; this packet is **the rest of the docket**:
the six working cohorts toad by toad (Pond Patrol 21, The Wardens 20, The
Crafters 16, The Menders 13, The Chroniclers 10, The Scouts 3) and a few
generic statblocks for the Barrel Survivors (4) and the Unassigned (2).

All eighty-nine are generated by
[`tools/build-liberated-toads-actors.py`](../../../tools/build-liberated-toads-actors.py)
from [`data/liberatedToadsSystem.json`](../../data/liberatedToadsSystem.json)
— **do not hand-edit**; change the docket data or the generator and re-run it
(`check-all` runs its `--check`). Fifteen docket rows are skipped on purpose
because they have articles and cast sheets already (Pondscum, Lilystalker,
Spearwort, Shieldback, Inkspot, Quillback, Gearspore, Hammerling, Soothing
Moss, Gentlepad, Shadowleap, Whispertail, Nerd T and both Geek T rows); the
generator prints them.

## Install

1. Copy `Reputation-Matrix2/portraits/liberated-toads/` to
   `<Foundry Data>/portraits/liberated-toads/` (nineteen 512-px PNGs with
   transparency — the docket's own role plates, keyed off their white field).
   Paths in the JSON are `portraits/liberated-toads/<plate>.png`. Or let the
   bridge do it:
   `python3 tools/foundry-bridge.py install-images Reputation-Matrix2/actors/liberated-toads --foundry-data "<Foundry Data>"`.
2. **One click:** with the
   [Waluipedia Mass Import module](../../Foundry/mass_import/README.md) enabled,
   Actors sidebar → **Import** → URL
   `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mikegent01/bik/gh-pages/Reputation-Matrix2/actors/liberated-toads/import.json`
   (or upload [`import.json`](import.json), or paste
   [`macros/import-liberated-toads.js`](../../Foundry/mass_import/macros/import-liberated-toads.js)
   into a script macro). It creates *Liberated Toads* with one sub-folder per
   cohort (*Pond Patrol*, *The Wardens*, *The Crafters*, *The Menders*, *The
   Chroniclers*, *The Scouts*, *Barrel Survivors*, *The Unassigned*, coloured
   as on the docket), all eighty-nine actors with their fixed `_id`s, and
   reports any token the server cannot find. Running it again after a rebuild
   **updates** them in place. You rarely need to: the sheets suite folds this
   packet into the world's everything-import exactly as it does the era
   packets (the folder scheme's `packets` names it), so a plain **Sync** brings
   the cohorts in with everything else.
3. Prototype tokens are unlinked, so three Sentry Ts are three HP pools. The
   whole packet is disposition *friendly* — they are the party's own faction.
   The generic Barrel Survivors and Unassigned are meant to be renamed on the
   token when a particular toad matters.

`import.json` is generated —
`python3 tools/foundry-bridge.py combine Reputation-Matrix2/actors/liberated-toads --out Reputation-Matrix2/actors/liberated-toads/import.json --world liberated-toads`
— and `check-all` fails when it drifts from the eighty-nine files. Each
actor's folder lives in `flags["waluipedia-mass-import"].folderPath`
(`["Liberated Toads", "<cohort>"]`); the `_id`s are derived from the slug, so
they never change between rebuilds. The docket row itself is kept on the
sheet as `flags["waluipedia-sheets"].docket` (cohort, role, level, note).

## How a docket row becomes a statblock

- **Level → CR.** A fighting cohort (Pond Patrol, The Wardens) runs 1/8, 1/4,
  1/2, 1, 2 for levels 1–5; a working cohort (Chroniclers, Crafters, Menders,
  Scouts, the generics) runs 0, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1. Proficiency follows the CR.
- **Class definition → feats.** The docket's `classDefinitions` (militia,
  fighter, artisan, commoner, spy, leader) list skills with a level; every
  skill the toad's level has reached becomes a feat carrying the docket's
  line and a table reading of it (*Shield Wall: +2 AC while adjacent to an
  ally wielding a shield*). Hit dice are the definition's (d8 militia, d10
  fighter, d6 artisan and commoner, d8 spy and leader).
- **Cohort → kit and a cohort feat.** Equipment is the cohort's (the
  Wardens' mail and spear, the Crafters' hammer and wrench …); fighting
  cohorts gain Multiattack at level 4. The cohort feat (*Pond Patrol
  Training*, *Wardens' Discipline*, *Crafters' Trade*, *Menders' Care*,
  *Chroniclers' Record*, *Scouts' Craft*) is the cohort's purpose as a rule.
- **Role → a feat named for the post.** *Door and deck watch*, *Speaker close
  escort*, *Propellant and powder*, *Trauma desk* — with the docket's note
  where there is one (<q>Says halt like a lifestyle.</q>) and a one-line
  mechanical perk keyed off the post (a watch cannot be surprised on post, a
  runner's Dash provokes no opportunity attacks, the powder room has
  advantage on saves against its own explosives …). The plate is chosen
  by the post too (`guard_pike` for a watch, `messenger_letter` for a desk,
  `medic_satchel` for a ward).
- **Biography** cites the docket row and the cohort's leader; tags are
  `Liberated Toads`, `npc`, the cohort, `humanoid`, `level N`, `docket` (and
  `generic` for the Barrel Survivors / Unassigned statblocks).

## Re-cutting the plates

`python3 tools/build-liberated-toads-actors.py plates` cuts the nineteen role
plates from `assets/images/toads/roster/field_*.png` through
`tools/make-token-plates.py`'s `cut()` (same keying as the era packets'
portraits). The plates are committed; re-cut only when the docket art changes.
