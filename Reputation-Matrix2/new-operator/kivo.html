📜 WHAT IS KIVOTOS?

Kivotos is a city-state of impossible scale—a vast metropolitan region covering hundreds of square kilometers, built upon and around a system of academies that function as the entire apparatus of civilization. There is no mayor. There is no parliament. There is no army—not exactly. There are only schools.

Each academy is simultaneously: a school, a government, a culture, a military, an economic bloc, a legal authority, and a way of life. To be a student is to be a citizen, a soldier, a constituent, and a believer—all at once.

The population of Kivotos is overwhelmingly composed of students—young women bearing geometric halos who carry weapons as standard equipment. Adult teachers are vanishingly rare. The role of external advisor—called Sensei—is critical to keeping the whole thing from collapsing entirely. Nobody knows exactly why this dependency exists. Nobody likes to talk about it.

⚡ THE RULES OF KIVOTOS

Kivotos operates on what external observers call "internal logic"—a set of rules that make sense within the city but baffle outsiders completely. Violence is constant but somehow calibrated. Students are shot, blown up, and buried under rubble daily. They recover. Buildings are destroyed. They are rebuilt. The city absorbs its own chaos like a living organism.

⚠ FIELD NOTE (Waluigi): This place makes NO sense. Students are carrying rocket launchers to class. One just fired a grenade at my food cart and then apologized very formally. The halo above her head turned slightly pink. WAH. I need a drink.

The precise mechanism of Kivotos resilience is unknown. Theories include: Halo-mediated reality stabilization, academy-wide engineering cycles, or simply that Kivotos refuses to acknowledge permanent damage. Whatever the reason, it works. Mostly.

💫 HALO MANIFESTATION

Every student in Kivotos manifests a halo—a geometric shape of light that floats above or around their head. Halos are not decorative. They are indicators:

  • Green/Blue halos — Student is healthy, functional, in good standing
  • Yellow/Gold halos — Student is stressed, conflicted, or under pressure
  • Red/Dark halos — Severe emotional or spiritual distress; possible "Plana" corruption
  • Fractured halos — Dimensional interference; Planar Fracture proximity
  • White/Brilliant halos — Peak condition; possible transcendent state

Halo shapes vary by student type. Circles, hexagons, crosses, fractals, and stranger geometries all appear. The shape is believed to relate to role, lineage, and purpose—though no student can choose their shape.

🌀 THE PLANAR FRACTURE

Approximately 14 months ago, reality cracked. Not everywhere. Not completely. But enough. A phenomenon called the Planar Fracture began manifesting throughout Kivotos—dimensional rifts that destabilize local reality, displace students across distances or even across world-regions, and interact strangely with halo energy.

⚠ CLASSIFIED: Several Millennium Science School students have gone missing in Fracture events and reportedly appeared in the Doughnut Hole. Three have been recovered. The others... are not confirmed lost. Yet.

The Fracture has made the ongoing inter-academy tensions dramatically worse. Resources are strained. Students displaced from their academies are technically "stateless" until re-enrolled. Some have formed independent cells. Some have been recruited by external factions operating out of region. The Cybernetic Pact has been particularly active in these zones.

📖 ORIGIN HISTORY — THE FOUNDING OF THE ACADEMIES
The academies of Kivotos did not emerge from a single governing vision. Each carries its own founding myth, its own reason for existing. Understanding the academies means understanding what they believed themselves to be—before the world complicated everything.
THE GREAT PACT (Est. ~200 years ago)

The original founding document of Kivotos established that academies would govern themselves without centralized authority. Each school would be sovereign. The General Student Union was created not as a government but as a forum—a place where academy representatives could argue without guns drawn. It has worked, imperfectly, for two centuries.
The first Sensei arrived approximately 50 years after the Great Pact. Nobody knows where they came from. The records describe only that the academies "needed guidance that students could not provide themselves" and that a figure from outside answered. This has been repeated periodically ever since.
THE DARK YEARS (Est. ~80 years ago)

A period of severe inter-academy warfare left entire districts ruined. The Rebuilding Accord that followed established the current coalition system—academies grouping into blocs for mutual protection. The Trinity General Grand Coalition and the older Shanhaijing agreements both trace to this era.
THE SENSEI DEPENDENCY CRISIS (Est. ~30 years ago)

For a 6-year period, no Sensei was present in Kivotos. What happened during those years is partially classified. What is known: academy conflict escalated severely, Abydos High School's founding population was nearly entirely lost, and the Gehenna Academy "incident" that burned three districts was never formally attributed to any party.

🎓 THE SENSEI ROLE — FIELD GUIDE

A Sensei is an external advisor to Kivotos—someone who arrives from outside the academy system and holds a unique position that students cannot fill themselves. The reasons for this dependency are debated. Leading theories:

  • Students within the system cannot see it clearly—too close to their own academy loyalties
  • The Sensei carries no halo, meaning they exist outside the status hierarchy that governs student interaction
  • External advisors provide cross-academy legitimacy that no single student faction can claim
  • Some researchers believe it is structural—that Kivotos was literally designed to require external input
PLAYER NOTE: In the current Grand Assembly crisis, your character may serve as or work alongside a Sensei figure. This gives extraordinary diplomatic access—but also makes you a target for every faction that believes the "right" Sensei would favor them. You have no weapons. You have no halo. What you have is everyone paying attention to you.

📜 THE HISTORY OF KIVOTOS — CONDENSED FOR GENERAL READING
PRE-FOUNDING ERA
Before the Great Pact // Estimated: 80-100 Years Ago

Kivotos did not begin as an academy city. The earliest records describe a settlement—or possibly multiple settlements—on what would become the Doughnut's interior facing surface, established by groups who arrived from different world-regions for reasons that are either not recorded or were deliberately obscured.

What is agreed upon: the halos appeared before the academies. Students in the pre-founding era already manifested the geometric halo formations above their heads, but without an institutional framework to explain or regulate them. The first recorded halo manifestation in a non-student (an adult advisor figure, later interpreted as a proto-Sensei) caused significant public alarm and is possibly the origin point of the Sensei dependency system, though this interpretation is contested.

"The lights above their heads were already there when we arrived. We built the schools around the lights, not the other way around."
— Attributed to one of the three founding figures of Trinity General School, identity disputed

Shanhaijing claims its lineage predates even this period. They have not provided evidence. They have also not denied the claim. The Shanhaijing position is, characteristically, to say nothing and let others argue about it.

FOUNDING ERA
The Great Pact // Approximately 60 Years Ago

The Great Pact formalized the academy system. Prior to the Pact, student groups operated without official recognition or territorial boundaries—conflicts were frequent, unregulated, and significantly more destructive than the managed conflicts of the modern era. The Pact established:

▸ Official academy recognition and territorial boundaries
▸ The General Student Union as a neutral arbiter
▸ The Sensei Coordination Protocol (see: Sensei System)
▸ Rules of armed engagement between academies (the so-called "No Permanent Death" conventions—how these work is not fully explained in the Pact text)
▸ The Halo Registration System, requiring all student halos to be documented
▸ Formal recognition of Kivotos as a distinct political entity within the Doughnut

The Great Pact was signed by eleven academy representatives. Two of those academies no longer exist. Three have changed their names. The Pact document is stored in the GSU Tower archive and has been amended forty-seven times, though amendment thirty-one is disputed and amendment forty-two was withdrawn under circumstances nobody discusses.

HISTORIAN'S NOTE: The Great Pact's "No Permanent Death" provisions are among the most studied and least understood clauses in Kivotos law. Students who are defeated in academy conflicts recover. Buildings that are destroyed are rebuilt. The mechanism for this is officially attributed to "halo resilience"—a term that appears in the Pact text but is never defined. Millennium has been trying to quantify it for thirty years. They have not succeeded.
THE DARK YEARS
Period of Instability // Approximately 35-45 Years Ago

The Dark Years represent the closest Kivotos has come to systemic collapse. The trigger is disputed—different academies have different official histories—but the consequences are agreed upon:

▸ The Grand Assembly was suspended. It has not been reconvened until now—eleven years after the most recent crisis, forty years after the Dark Years
▸ Multiple Sensei figures disappeared or withdrew simultaneously. The Dependency Crisis followed
▸ Several academies that existed before the Dark Years do not exist afterward
▸ Abydos High School, which was apparently prosperous before the Dark Years, emerged from them in its current near-defunct state
▸ Valkyrie Police Academy was established specifically as a post-Dark-Years institution

The Dark Years are taught differently in each academy. Trinity frames them as a period of necessary purification. Red Winter frames them as an example of what happens when individual academies prioritize self-interest. Millennium doesn't teach them at all in official curriculum—the period is labeled "Historical Data Gap" in their records.

WHAT CAUSED THE DARK YEARS: No official account has been formally adopted by the GSU. The three leading theories are: (1) coordinated external interference in Kivotos' political structure, (2) a halo resonance event that disrupted student decision-making, (3) the specific actions of one individual whose name does not appear in any official record. Shanhaijing knows which theory is correct. They have not said.
MODERN ERA
Post-Reconstruction // 35 Years Ago to Present

Kivotos rebuilt. The Coalition formed. The Cybernetic Pact emerged as a rival. Kaiser PMC established its loan operations. The Freelancer Underworld consolidated under the Broker's coordination. Academy conflicts returned to their "manageable" state—blocks destroyed, students defeated, borders disputed, but nothing approaching the scale of the Dark Years.

Eleven years ago, a smaller crisis—the details of which are classified at Coalition level—resulted in the last Sensei's withdrawal and the suspension of Assembly proceedings that had been building toward a new Fracture response protocol. That protocol was never completed. The Fracture has been expanding for eleven years without a coordinated response.

CURRENT STATUS: Day 3 of the first Grand Assembly in eleven years. A new Sensei has arrived. The Fracture is expanding. Thirteen factions are in the same room. So far, only three armed incidents. Things are, relatively speaking, going well.
💫 THE HALO SYSTEM — COMPREHENSIVE FIELD GUIDE
WHAT IS A HALO?

A halo is a geometric light formation that appears above the head of every Kivotos student. They are not decorative. They are not chosen. They manifest spontaneously during what appears to be an enrollment-adjacent process—students who attempt to attend a Kivotos academy without a halo find that they develop one within the first three weeks of term, or they don't remain enrolled.

The leading theory, attributed to Millennium's Department of Anomalous Phenomena, is that halos are "externalized cognitive-spiritual architecture"—they represent some internal state of the student made manifest in light. This theory explains several observed behaviors (halos flickering during emotional distress, brightening during moments of clarity or conviction) but does not explain shape variation, color coding, or why the halos of students who go through Planar Fracture events sometimes change permanently.

HALO RESILIENCE — THE KIVOTOS SURVIVAL MECHANISM

The most consequential and least understood function of halos is what students call "Kivotos resilience" and researchers call "halo-mediated recovery"—the phenomenon by which Kivotos students survive damage that should be fatal, recover from injuries that should be permanent, and continue functioning through circumstances that by any external medical standard should have concluded them.

This does not make students invincible. Defeat in combat is real, painful, and consequential for reputation and capability. But the permanent, irreversible consequences of conflict that exist in other world-regions are, in Kivotos, buffered by something the halos are doing that nobody has fully quantified.

EXCEPTION — PLANAR FRACTURE ZONES: Inside active Fracture zones, halo resilience does not function normally. Damage sustained within a Fracture zone may be permanent. This is one of the primary reasons the Fracture is classified as a crisis rather than a routine anomaly.
MILLENNIUM RESEARCH NOTE (PARTIAL DECLASSIFICATION): Our seventeen-year study of halo resilience has produced three conclusions: (1) The mechanism is real and measurable. (2) It is connected to the Sensei coordination system in ways we cannot fully map. (3) Our best current theory is that the halos are connected to something external to Kivotos that provides the energy for recovery. We do not know what that something is. We are trying to find out. Progress has been [REDACTED].
🎓 THE SENSEI SYSTEM — CLASSIFIED OVERVIEW
WHAT IS A SENSEI?

A Sensei is an external advisor—someone who comes from outside Kivotos (or at minimum from outside the academy structure) and provides coordination, guidance, and what the Dependency Protocol calls "the necessary external perspective." They are not teachers in the conventional sense. They do not set curriculum. They do not govern academies. What they do is considerably more difficult to define.

The closest functional description: a Sensei is a person whose presence allows Kivotos students to function at a higher level of cooperative capability. With a Sensei, academies negotiate better, fight more strategically, make decisions with longer time horizons, and experience measurably improved halo stability. Without a Sensei, the system tends toward short-term conflict, factional escalation, and the kind of catastrophic decision-making that characterized the Dark Years.

"We don't need someone to tell us what to do. We need someone to stand in the room while we figure out what to do. The difference matters."
— Red Winter Federal Academy, internal governance document on Sensei protocol
THE DEPENDENCY — HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS

The Sensei Dependency is not fully understood even by those who have studied it longest. What is documented:

▸ Halo Stabilization: The presence of an active Sensei correlates with measurably more stable halo resonance across student populations. The correlation is strong enough that Millennium uses it as a proxy indicator for Sensei presence.
▸ Coordination Enhancement: Cross-academy cooperation, which is difficult at baseline, becomes significantly more achievable when a Sensei is mediating. This is not because the Sensei gives orders—they don't. The mechanism is more subtle and more contested.
▸ Escalation Dampening: Conflict between academies tends toward de-escalation when a Sensei is present. Not always. But measurably. The Dark Years and the eleven-year gap both occurred during periods without an active Sensei.
▸ The Trust Mechanism: Students trust Senseis in a way that does not fully map onto normal authority relationships. This is not blind obedience—students regularly disagree with, ignore, and challenge Sensei guidance. But the relationship is different from any other authority in Kivotos.
THE DEPENDENCY CRISIS: When Sensei figures are absent, the system does not simply operate less efficiently—it begins to degrade. The Dark Years coincided with a period of simultaneous Sensei absence. The current eleven-year gap in Assembly proceedings coincided with the previous Sensei's departure. The relationship between Sensei absence and Kivotos instability is statistically significant. Nobody officially admits this.
WHAT SENSEIS KNOW — AND DON'T KNOW

Senseis arrive in Kivotos without complete information. This is, apparently, intentional. They learn the system by operating within it. The process of a Sensei orienting themselves to Kivotos—learning the academies, building relationships, understanding the politics—appears to be as important as their eventual coordination role.

Senseis who have served longest hold, by the end of their tenure, knowledge of Kivotos that exceeds that of any individual academy's leadership. What they do with that knowledge varies. What happens to it after they leave is unclear.

SHANHAIJING'S POSITION: Shanhaijing holds records on every Sensei who has operated in Kivotos going back to what they claim is the pre-founding era. They have not shared these records. When pressed, their senior students say, with complete sincerity, that "the records exist to be used at the appropriate time." When that time is has never been specified.
🌀 THE PLANAR FRACTURE — THREAT ASSESSMENT
WHAT IS THE PLANAR FRACTURE?

The Planar Fracture is an ongoing dimensional anomaly affecting Kivotos and adjacent regions of the Doughnut. It manifests as visible tears in the local spatial-dimensional fabric—zones where the normal rules of Kivotos physics are altered, disrupted, or absent. The Fracture has been expanding for at least twenty years, though the rate of expansion has accelerated in the last eleven.

Within Fracture zones: halo resilience is compromised or absent, dimensional displacement of personnel and objects occurs, physical laws behave inconsistently, and technology—particularly Millennium-standard technology—malfunctions in characteristic ways. Students who spend extended time in Fracture zones without protection report perceptual disturbances, temporal displacement (experiencing events out of sequence), and halo color/shape alterations.

CRITICAL DISTINCTION: Unlike normal Kivotos combat, injuries sustained within an active Fracture zone may not recover through standard halo resilience. This makes Fracture zones categorically more dangerous than any academy conflict, regardless of the weapons involved.

💰 THE KIVOTOS ECONOMY — HOW A CITY OF STUDENTS FUNCTIONS FINANCIALLY
OVERVIEW — THE PARADOX OF STUDENT GOVERNANCE

Kivotos is economically functional. This is not obvious when you first look at it—a city governed by teenagers, with no adult population to speak of, running infrastructure, finance, commerce, and military operations simultaneously. And yet it works. Not well, not smoothly, not without constant crisis, but it works.

The reason is that Kivotos students are not, in practice, teenagers in the conventional external-world sense. They have halo-enhanced cognitive function, institutional frameworks that channel energy into productive operation, and a cultural expectation that competence is required regardless of age. A Millennium engineer who is fifteen years old builds things that an adult from Earth Land with a full engineering degree might not manage. This is not explained. It is simply true.

CURRENCY AND TRADE
OFFICIAL CURRENCY: KIVOTOS CREDITS (KC)

Issued by the GSU Financial Administration. Accepted across all academies. Backed by a reserve that consists of: actual precious metals (Shanhaijing holds the primary reserve), technology patents (Millennium), and real estate claims (Trinity). The math works out, roughly. Do not ask for a detailed balance sheet.

SHADOW ECONOMY: GEHENNA EXCHANGE

A parallel economy operated primarily by Gehenna-affiliated networks. Uses informal credit systems, favor exchange, and physical goods. Significantly larger than official KC trade in some sectors—specifically logistics, information brokerage, and anything that requires moving through academy borders without documentation. The GSU officially does not recognize it. The GSU unofficially uses it when necessary.

KAISER PMC — THE CREDITOR PROBLEM

Kaiser PMC's Kivotos division operates as a loan and debt enforcement service. They provide financing to academies that cannot meet operational costs through standard GSU allocation. Their terms are competitive on paper. In practice, several small academies are in debt structures that cannot be resolved through normal operation—the debt grows faster than the academy can service it.

Abydos is the most visible case. Others exist. The GSU has passed three resolutions over twenty years calling for debt structure reform. Kaiser PMC has complied with none of them on the grounds that the resolutions lack enforcement mechanisms. This is correct. The GSU has no enforcement mechanism for Kaiser PMC because Kaiser PMC has never done anything that constitutes a violation of the Great Pact—they simply loan money and enforce repayment. That this results in territorial loss for small academies is, technically, legal.

CURRENT STATUS: Kaiser PMC has applied for Assembly observer status. If granted, they gain diplomatic cover for their operations during any crisis period the Assembly might declare. This would effectively shield them from emergency debt suspension measures. Blocking this without creating a legal precedent that could be used against the GSU's own creditors is the current problem for GSU Parliamentary staff.
🎭 KIVOTOS CULTURE — WHAT IT IS ACTUALLY LIKE TO LIVE HERE
DAILY LIFE IN KIVOTOS

For most students on most days, Kivotos is not a war zone. It is a city. There are clubs, cafeterias, libraries, competitive gaming lounges, after-school markets, and the particular social complexity of any large group of people who are required to spend significant time together in a defined space.

The difference from an external observer's perspective is the presence of weapons in what would otherwise be routine social situations: the student reading in the library with a rifle propped against the chair. The debate club whose members are wearing tactical vests. The food market where half the vendors are also clearly carrying sidearms. This is normal to Kivotos students. They have never not known this.

"The weapons aren't the point. The point is that the weapons are there and everyone knows they're there and everyone has chosen, today, not to use them. That's what civilization looks like here. It's a choice you make every morning."
— Valkyrie Police Academy, Training Manual, Chapter One

INTER-ACADEMY RELATIONS — THE SOCIAL LAYER

Students from different academies interact constantly—at Assembly events, in neutral zones, through the shadow economy, and through the complicated personal relationships that form when you put thousands of people with significant capability and energy into proximity. These relationships exist alongside and often in tension with formal academy rivalries.

It is entirely normal for a Millennium student and a Trinity student to be genuinely close friends whose respective academies are in formal diplomatic dispute. It is also normal for those students to be on opposite sides of a combat scenario the following week and resume the friendship afterward. Kivotos students distinguish between personal and institutional conflict in ways that external observers find either admirable or baffling depending on their perspective.

🗺 KIVOTOS GEOGRAPHY — DISTRICT OVERVIEW
THE CITY STRUCTURE

Kivotos occupies a significant portion of the Doughnut's interior-facing surface in this region—a city that has grown organically around the academy territories established by the Great Pact. There is no conventional urban planning agency. Territory is controlled by academies. Infrastructure is maintained by whoever controls the territory it sits on. The result is a city of dramatic contrasts: Millennium's precisely engineered districts adjacent to Abydos' deteriorating infrastructure, Trinity's maintained historic architecture next to Gehenna's improvised expansion zones.

CENTRAL DISTRICT
Controlled by: GSU (Nominal) / Contested
Home to the GSU Tower, the Grand Assembly Hall, and the city's primary commercial zone. Technically neutral. In practice, the district that every major faction maintains permanent presence in for "diplomatic purposes." The most heavily armed neutral zone in any world-region.
GSU Tower Grand Assembly Hall Central Market Valkyrie Precinct 1
TRINITY DISTRICT
Controlled by: Trinity General School
The oldest continuously maintained district in Kivotos. Stone buildings, formal architecture, maintained green spaces. The archives are here—the largest official historical repository in the city. Also the highest density of students carrying ceremonial weapons that are absolutely also functional weapons.
Trinity General Campus Grand Archives St. Aequitas Cathedral Coalition HQ
MILLENNIUM ZONE
Controlled by: Millennium Science School
The most vertical district in Kivotos. Millennium Tower dominates the skyline. Below it: research facilities, experimental zones, and a significant number of areas marked "AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY" whose authorization criteria have never been clearly defined. Currently has an active Class IV Fracture zone in its sub-levels.
Millennium Tower Research Complex Tech Marketplace ⚠ Fracture Zone (Sub-Level)
GEHENNA TERRITORY
Controlled by: Gehenna Academy
The most economically active zone in Kivotos by shadow market metrics. Gehenna Territory is where things get done that can't get done elsewhere. It is also where people get lost who are not careful. The district has excellent restaurants and a mortality-adjacent reputation that is somewhat exaggerated but not entirely.
Gehenna Academy Shadow Exchange Logistics Hub The Underground (multiple floors)
ABYDOS TERRITORY
Controlled by: Abydos High School (disputed)
The most sparsely populated district in Kivotos. Abydos territory has been described as "a desert in the middle of a city" which is geographically accurate and metaphorically apt. Infrastructure is failing. Territory is being encroached upon. The adjacent Class III Fracture zone has been doing something unusual for eight months. There are six students and one functioning heating system.
Abydos High School ⚠ Perimeter Fracture Zone Abandoned Residential (multiple) Desert Training Ground
THE BORDERLANDS
Controlled by: No One / Everyone
The zones between formal academy territories. Officially no-man's land. In practice: where the Freelancer Underworld operates most freely, where cross-academy black markets function, where students who don't fit their academies find community, and where the most interesting and most dangerous things in Kivotos tend to occur.

01
WHAT ACTUALLY CAUSED THE DARK YEARS
UNSOLVED

Three competing theories. All three have evidence supporting them. All three have evidence that should rule them out. The leading candidate involves an individual whose name has been systematically removed from Kivotos historical records. Shanhaijing has the records. Shanhaijing has not shared them. The individual may or may not still be alive. The individual may or may not be connected to the Planar Fracture. These are separate questions. Possibly.

KNOWN:
Multiple Sensei figures withdrew simultaneously. Multiple academies collapsed. Something reset the balance.
UNKNOWN:
Why. By whom. Whether it was deliberate. Whether it can happen again.
02
THE ORIGIN OF HALO MANIFESTATION
UNSOLVED

Halos appear on students who enroll in Kivotos academies. They appear regardless of the student's origin—students from Earth Land, Animatopia, the Internet, and elsewhere all manifest halos within weeks of enrollment. This suggests the halos are not inherent to students but are something Kivotos does to them. What Kivotos is and why it does this is not answered by any official document.

KNOWN:
Halos are real. They have measurable effects. They connect to the Sensei system somehow.
UNKNOWN:
Source. Mechanism. Whether they can be removed. Whether removing them would be survivable.
03
THE ABYDOS COLLAPSE
PARTIAL EVIDENCE

Abydos was prosperous. Something happened during the Dark Years or the Dependency Crisis that reduced it to its current state. The records are fragmentary. Trinity General has sealed records related to Abydos. The Abydos Perimeter Fracture is behaving unusually—specifically, it appears to be maintaining a boundary rather than expanding. This is not standard Fracture behavior. The Fracture may be protecting something in the Abydos school that it knows about and we don't.

KNOWN:
Abydos was different before. Trinity sealed something. The Fracture is anomalous.
UNKNOWN:
What collapsed Abydos. What Trinity sealed. What the Fracture is protecting. Whether these are related.
04
SECTOR SEVEN
CLASSIFIED

A Class V Fracture zone that does not appear on official maps. Shanhaijing maps it. The Freelancer Underworld routes around it. Millennium knows it exists. Something is on the other side of it. No current technology can breach a Class V Fracture. This may be intentional. The question of what is on the other side and who put it there is not one that any official body has formally asked, possibly because asking would require acknowledging that Sector Seven exists.

KNOWN:
It exists. Multiple parties know about it. Nobody is investigating it officially.
UNKNOWN:
Everything else.
05
THE PREVIOUS SENSEI'S DEPARTURE
PARTIAL EVIDENCE

The previous Sensei left eleven years ago. The official record says "withdrawal for undisclosed reasons." The actual circumstances involved an Assembly session that was suspended mid-vote on something classified at Coalition level. Several students who were present in the room that day have been informally relocated to non-sensitive positions across multiple academies. They don't discuss it. The new Sensei is arriving with no briefing on what happened. This may have been deliberate.

KNOWN:
The departure was sudden. It was connected to the suspended Assembly. People who were there don't talk.
UNKNOWN:
What was being voted on. Why the Sensei left. Whether they were asked to leave or chose to.
06
WHAT SHANHAIJING ACTUALLY KNOWS
ONGOING

Shanhaijing holds records of everything. Their archive predates the Great Pact. Their senior students react to information in ways that suggest they already know it. Their delegates at the Assembly vote consistently and correctly on questions that other delegations are still researching. They have never been wrong about a Kivotos historical question. They have never fully explained how they know what they know. The archive theft (Scenario 05) suggests someone else wants to know too.

KNOWN:
They know more than they say. Their records are older than anything else in Kivotos. Someone broke in to steal from them.
UNKNOWN:
The full extent. What the theft took. Whether Shanhaijing will ever share voluntarily.

I find this both troubling and completely consistent with every other region I have visited. The Doughnut contains many places. All of them have secrets. Kivotos has more secrets than most and is also running them at a significantly younger average age than most. WAH. I respect this. I do not fully understand it. WAH.
