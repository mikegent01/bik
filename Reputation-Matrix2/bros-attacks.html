Bros Attacks

A Bros Attack is a coordinated maneuver in which two allies spend timing, positioning, and trust to produce an effect neither could reliably make alone.

Two techniques are currently confirmed. Waluigi has filed both with the usual caution: a successful maneuver can still serve a terrible idea.

Confirmed · Ranged support fire

Support Fire Bros Attack

Green T and Remi

Aim correctionTiming callFirearm

One partner steadies the shooter’s aim, adjusts her position, and calls the timing so the shot lands with greater force and accuracy. Green T taught Remi from a simple instruction sheet. Remi immediately used it against the tanuki behind a barricaded bathroom door.

Steps

  1. Choose the shooter and the support partner.
  2. Correct stance, sightline, and timing.
  3. Wait for the call instead of firing on impulse.
  4. Resolve the shot, then account for its consequences.

WAH! Green T turned mentorship into a firearms incident before the ink dried. That is not a failure of coordination. That is coordination working for the wrong sentence.

Confirmed · Close-range clearing

Chop Bros

Hjumpik and Toad Lee

LiftAxe rhythmVine clearing

Hjumpik lifts Toad Lee to vine-height while Toad Lee chops through the growth ahead. The first attempt failed on weight. The second nearly produced friendly fire after a countdown. The third worked when they stopped counting and found a shared rhythm.

Result

Twenty-five feet of corridor opened. A large amount of leaf growth was pushed back. The party reached the window and climbed upward at the end of the engagement.

Steps

  1. Establish a stable lift.
  2. Keep the axe clear of Hjumpik and nearby allies.
  3. Move by rhythm rather than a shouted countdown.
  4. Stop before exhaustion turns the lift into a drop.

WAH! Attempt One failed on weight. Attempt Two failed on numbers. Attempt Three succeeded when the bros started listening to each other. I hate how instructive this is.
