Permanent Injury Table

100 HAND-AUTHORED ROWS · LOW IS WORSE · A d100 table for injuries that outlast the fight: 1 is death, the twenties are the body never being the same, the fifties heal in weeks, the nineties are the luck of the survivor. Roll when the fiction calls for it, read the lowest cure, and keep the result attached to the character rather than buried in a session transcript. Tiers: pick the table by what did the harm — the default for steel and claw, Venom & Web for bites and cocoons, Fire & Blast for anything that cooked or threw you. Every tier is 100 hand-authored rows and no name or mechanic repeats across tables.
