🦙 The Gift Shop

Tap to earn coins, then spend them on merchandise of famously inconsistent quality. At least one item on this shelf is a straightforward swindle. That is not a bug; it is who you are buying from.
The Ward. Out on the floor the house decides what happens to you. In here you get a say, briefly. Roll a d20 at the start of each turn while dying: 10 or higher is a success. Three successes and you stabilise, three failures and you do not. A natural 1 costs two failures. A natural 20 puts you straight back on your feet at 1 hit point — the only outright win in the building.
