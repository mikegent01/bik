Archie's ice magic doesn't just cool the room — it slows time itself within the circle. Without him, the ritual tears itself apart from temporal friction.

Clear mind of fire-related thoughts

▶ ⏳ TIMELINE SHEAR (Early Interruption)

Trigger:

Oracle interrupted BEFORE mirrors go dull (before Scene 6)

WORST ENDING TERRITORY:
  • • Reality fractures around the circle
  • • Time loops possible
  • • Erasure of participants possible
Combat in Fractured Time:
  • • All attacks have 50% miss chance (d20, 1-10 miss)
  • • Spells may loop (cast twice, hit self)
  • • Movement unpredictable (roll d4 for direction)
  • • Death doesn't stick (but neither does victory)
Only Escape:

Oracle must speak the true-name anyway, while fighting. DC 25 Concentration check per round of combat.
