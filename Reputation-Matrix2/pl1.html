• Usk has been brought in - beaten, bloody, the "guest of honor"

⚠ WHY PLAYERS SHOULD CARE

  • • The vote buys TIME. While they debate, Green T gets into position on the balcony.
  • • The Oracle wants Orangus dead - to "sever the family tree" and merge the timelines.

🔮 SECRETS, DIRT & LEVERAGE

Information discovered during the dinner. Each can be weaponized during the vote or combat.

The Oracle's True Plan

LEVERAGE: CRITICAL

The Oracle wants to "sever the family tree" - kill all heirs, leave one keeper per manor. But Perrius says the crystals keep the monster IN, not OUT. Is the Oracle lying?

If revealed: Chaos. Who do the players trust? The Oracle or the Mages Guild?

Archie's Identity

LEVERAGE: HIGH

Archie is impersonating Darman Knightly. The REAL Darman is at the table. Vaxillus suspects something. One wrong move and the cover is blown.

If revealed: Vaxillus attacks. The real Darman either helps or condemns Archie.

Toadburt's Ring

LEVERAGE: MODERATE

Toadburt was fiddling with a Mages Guild dampening ring. Now it's GONE. Someone took it - or he hid it. He's an "esteemed guest" but clearly nervous.

If revealed: Orangus questions his loyalty. The Mages Guild's involvement is exposed.

Green T's Plan

LEVERAGE: CRITICAL

Green T told Archie: "When the big lid opens, hit the deck." He has a pistol. He stole Orangus's key. He's planning to shoot the crystal and trigger the table flip.

If revealed: Guards swarm Green T. The ambush is ruined. Combat starts badly.

Franklin's Mission

LEVERAGE: MEDIUM

Franklin is a Mages Guild representative here to "observe." But Perrius (also Mages Guild) says the crystals keep something IN. What is Franklin really after?

If revealed: Franklin may panic. He might have a teleport scroll ready.

Usk's Past

LEVERAGE: MODERATE

Usk was mentioned in the note Remi found: "dear Usk... truly faithful confidant and ally. I will gravely miss him when the time comes." He knows about the vessel.

If revealed: Usk might share crucial information about the crystal's true purpose.

The Rotten Food

LEVERAGE: LOW

Remi and Markop (from the Atrium) see ash and maggots on the plates. The illusion is failing for them. The food Archie ate had blood mixed in.

If revealed: Guests panic. The manor's magic is weakening.

The Encased Monster

LEVERAGE: ULTIMATE

Markop and Remi found a monster in a magical case near the Dreaming Tree. It's frozen, protected by Abjuration magic, with Necromancy aura. Its claw is reaching out of the barrier.

If revealed: This might be what Perrius warned about. Breaking crystals might release it.

🗳 PHASE 3: THE VOTE - Blood Ballot
"Slips of parchment appear before each guest, along with quills that seem to write in dried blood. Orangus spreads his hands.

'The rules are simple. Write a name—any name at this table. The one with the most votes will... demonstrate their commitment to our cause. Abstaining is, of course, an option. But I do so hate fence-sitters.'

His eyes sweep the room. 'You have one minute. Choose wisely. Choose quickly. Choose... entertainingly.'"
GM NOTE: Actually give players a time limit. 60 seconds. This is where Green T signals Archie and slips toward the window/balcony. The real Darman is watching Archie write.
🔮 PHASE 4: DESSERT - The Crystal Revealed
"The waiters return, placing silver domes before each guest. A massive, chest-sized platter is set in the center. Orangus stands.

'We have spoken of the past. Now, let us look to the future. I call this course... Manifestation. Eat what you are due.'

You lift your lids. There is no food. And then Orangus lifts the center dome—and you see it. The Orange Crystal. Pulsing. Screaming. Wired into the severed head of a Warforged. Reality cracks at its edges."
INDIVIDUAL PLATES:
• Archie: A single, fresh human eye. It blinks at him. (Reference to his third eye?)
• Bowser: A miniature frozen statue of himself, cracked and broken. Smells like sulfur.
• Toadburt: Empty plate with a bleeding Iron Legion badge.
• Remi/Markop (watching from Atrium): They see ash and maggots on everyone's plates. The illusion has completely failed for them.
⚔ PHASE 5: THE TABLE FLIP - Combat Begins
"A shot rings out from above. Green T stands on the balcony, pistol smoking, shouting: 'DINNER IS CANCELED! ARCHIE, NOW!'

The crystal screams. The table flips. Wood and silver crash as a shockwave of temporal energy explodes outward. The wall behind you CRACKS—and through the breach, you see Markop and Remi in the Fractured Atrium, weapons drawn, reality tearing open between the two spaces.

Orangus floats above the chaos, his mask of civility gone. 'You have broken my china,' he hisses. 'Now I will break your bones.'"
GM NOTE: ROLL INITIATIVE. The Dining Hall and Fractured Atrium have MERGED. Green T is on the balcony (high ground). Orangus is airborne. The table provides half cover. Light from the Atrium hurts vampires. The encased monster near the Dreaming Tree—is it stirring?
🌀 THE CONVERGENCE - Two Rooms Become One
"The shockwave hits the walls of reality. From the Fractured Atrium, Markop and Remi see the 'glass' viewing window begin to crack from the inside. They see Archie and Bowser flipping the table in slow motion.

The Oracle's voice echoes: 'The barrier is thin! If you want to save them, you must break it from this side!'

The glass shatters. The two rooms violently merge. Gravity goes sideways for a moment. Debris flies everywhere. The vampires hiss as REAL LIGHT from the Atrium floods their shadow dimension. Colors that shouldn't exist bleed between spaces."
POST-EXPLOSION POSITIONING:
• Center: Overturned dining table, smoking. Archie, Bowser, Toadburt behind it (half cover).
• High Ground: Green T on the balcony, reloading his pistol.
• The Breach: Jagged hole in reality. Markop and Remi standing there, weapons drawn.
• Enemies: Orangus floating above. Vivesna hissing. Vaxillus drawing his blade. Guards at the doors.

🩸 AFTERMATH: What Happens After Each Vote Result

The vote's outcome shapes the combat and future story. Each result has immediate, combat, and long-term consequences.

If ORANGUS won the vote...

Immediate: Orangus is ENRAGED but impressed. "You dare?" He burns the ballots with purple flame. The vote is annulled.

Combat Effect: Orangus gains +1 Legendary Action. He focuses whoever nominated him first. That player has disadvantage on saves against his abilities for 1 round.

Long-term: If Orangus survives, he places a Blood Curse on the voter. They're marked for assassination across all timelines.

If ARCHIE (as Darman) won the vote...

Immediate: Orangus slides the knife to Archie. "You claim to be Darman... or whoever you are. Carve the traitor Usk, or offer your own hand." The REAL Darman watches intently.

Combat Effect: If Archie refuses, combat starts immediately. If Archie attacks Usk, Green T HESITATES (loses first turn). The real Darman's reaction depends on Archie's choice.

Long-term: The real Darman Knightly will remember. He becomes ally (grateful Archie didn't sully his name further) or enemy (furious at the impersonation).

If BOWSER won the vote...

Immediate: Vivesna sneers with delight. "The loud one. The rude one." Orangus nods. "Gut the prisoner Usk, or we mount your head next to the moose."

Combat Effect: Bowser will REFUSE and RAGE. He breathes fire immediately. Advantage on first attack. Vivesna (who nominated him) is his priority target.

Long-term: The Vampire Court now sees Bowser as "the beast who ruined dinner." He has a reputation. Future vampire encounters start hostile.

If USK won the vote...

Immediate: Orangus sighs. "Predictable. Boring. You vote for the dead man walking?" He randomly assigns an executioner from the players (roll 1d4).

Combat Effect: The assigned player must use their first action to either attack Usk OR openly defy Orangus. No other options. This forces the moral dilemma.

Long-term: If Usk survives, he owes the party a life debt. He knows secrets about the Crystal's origin and the "vessel" mentioned in the note.

If TOADBURT won the vote...

Immediate: Toadburt freezes, blood draining from his face. Orangus tosses the knife. "Kill your friend Usk. Do it, and you may live to see dessert."

Combat Effect: Toadburt has 6 seconds (1 round) to decide. If players don't intervene, he WILL try to kill Usk to save himself. GREEN T WILL NOT FORGIVE THIS.

Long-term: Toadburt's loyalty is permanently determined. Saved by players = ally for life. Killed Usk = Green T marks him as a traitor to the Toads.

If GREEN T won the vote...

Immediate: Green T refuses instantly. He pulls his pistol. "I don't think so, fangs." He signals Archie and combat starts EARLY.

Combat Effect: Green T gets a SURPRISE ROUND. However, the Crystal hasn't been revealed yet—they'll have to find it during combat. Dessert phase is skipped.

Long-term: Green T's cover is blown. The Toad rebellion loses a key operative. But his legend grows among the resistance.

If VIVESNA won the vote...

Immediate: Orangus looks at his daughter. The room goes silent. "Ambitious," he whispers. "But not today." He crushes the ballots. Vote is VOID.

Combat Effect: Vivesna is HUMILIATED. Roll d6: 1-3 she focuses players who voted for her, 4-6 she hesitates to help Orangus (loses 1 turn).

Long-term: Vivesna's coup plans accelerate. If Orangus dies, she blames the party. If he lives, she blames him. Either way, she becomes a recurring villain.

If THE REAL DARMAN won the vote...

Immediate: Darman stands slowly. "Interesting. The impostor's friends vote for ME." He looks at Archie. "We should talk. After I deal with this knife situation."

Combat Effect: Darman may fight WITH the party or AGAINST them, depending on how Archie has handled the impersonation. Roll d6: 1-3 hostile, 4-6 curious ally.

Long-term: If Darman survives and is an ally, the party gains a powerful friend. If hostile, they've made a dangerous enemy who knows Archie's secret.

If there was a TIE...

Immediate: Orangus forces the tied candidates to fight each other. Or he kills the Waiter to break the tie. "Problem solved."

Combat Effect: Chaos. One NPC may be dead or injured before initiative. Alliances are confused—some NPCs don't know whose side they're on.

Long-term: The "Tie-Breaker Incident" becomes infamous. The dinner where democracy failed.

🔮 THE ORANGE CRYSTAL IN COMBAT

The Orange Crystal screams continuously (concentration checks DC 12 for spellcasters within 30ft).

Each round, roll d6:

  • 1-2: Time hiccup - one random creature gets an extra action
  • 3-4: Temporal echo - a ghostly copy of someone from the Atrium appears for 1 round
  • 5: Reality crack - 10ft radius teleports between Dining Hall and Atrium randomly
  • 6: The crystal PULSES - everyone makes DC 15 CON save or stunned for 1 round

To destroy it: AC 18, 50 HP, immune to psychic/necrotic.

WARNING: Perrius said the crystals keep something IN. Destroying it might release the encased monster.
