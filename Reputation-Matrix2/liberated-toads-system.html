Liberated Toads: Faction Status Report

Chapter Retelling: “The Mirrors, the Ghosts, and the Fall”

A Dark-Fantasy Chronicle of Bowser, Hjumpik, and the Toad Cohort

The Return Through the Manor

Hjumpik insists they go back for the missing toads — Toad Lee and Toadburt — and Bowser grudgingly agrees, muttering that toads are idiots but they’re his idiots at the moment.

In a warped hallway mirror, a Mirror Stalker glints into view. Hjumpik waves nervously at it and the creature silently blinks out of existence. A toad screams somewhere deeper in the manor.

They sprint.

Bowser is already scooping up a limp toad — Toad Lee — nudging him like a disgruntled babysitter. Toad Lee can only manage, “One of us ran… the other went to fight the mirror monster head-on…”

Hjumpik orders Bowser to go after the brave idiot. Bowser mutters that if anything happens to Hjumpik, someone better tell him, because he’s terrible at caring. Then he squeezes his spiked shoulders through a doorway that clearly wasn’t meant for anything that size.

The Seneschal’s Quarters

The room is heavy with a sinking dread.

Mirrored screens divide the space in elegant ruins. Armchairs face each other in an oval, as if the ghosts of nobles still debate here. A collapse in the west has sealed the door permanently.

Hjumpik checks the mirrors — no monster.

Toad Lee suggests an escape window. Hjumpik refuses; he’s seen too many things in reflections today.

They search the adjacent bedrooms. One is empty. The other hosts the Oracle, rambling excessively about tending to bathrooms, the fate of the manor, how orcs invaded, how it slipped between worlds, how mirrors once had rules… Hjumpik gives up mid-sentence and leaves him to mutter to the dust.

Bowser and the Green Toad

Meanwhile, Bowser is gripping the hand of a green toad trapped in a mirror. Green-T’s reflection stares back, mouth moving but with no sound. Bowser calls him “mushroom head” with what passes for affection.

Dan suggests they go into the mirror to fight the monster. Bowser calls him expendable.

When Bowser tries to reposition his grip on the mirror, the Mirror Stalker slashes him from the reflection. Bowser snarls and staggers.

The Rooftop Struggle

On the roof, Toad Lee emerges dragging the other missing toad, Toadburt, who’s barely conscious.

Hjumpik orders them inside — the roof is too dangerous. He jams his warhammer into the warped frame of the door and forces it open… which accidentally knocks Toadburt off balance. The poor toad tumbles into the tangle of dead weeds below but manages to cling to a ledge.

Cut back to Bowser: Bloodied, annoyed, he announces they’re leaving now and that Dan is weak. Dan calls Bowser weak. Bowser responds by picking him up and sprinting through the hall.

He finds the Oracle and demands to know how to free Green-T. The Oracle gives a cryptic answer: “Once the three demons are expelled, the manor returns to a simpler time — without mirrors.”

He then hands Bowser a skull-marked key but warns it’s kept in the Solarium — where the Mirror Monster nests.

Bowser growls. Too much talking. He leaves.

The Regroup and the Ghosts’ Show

Hjumpik hauls Toadburt back to safety. Everyone regroups. Bowser briefs them:

Two options:
– Return to the dangerous tightrope room, or
– Go to the Solarium where the monster lurks.

They trade insults. Bowser calls Hjumpik a three-eyed chump. Hjumpik calls Bowser a three-eyed fireball. Dan agrees with whoever seems least likely to kill him.

Hjumpik lays out a plan: reach the Piano Room, use the warhammer as a shock-absorber, catch the toads. “Except you, Bowser. You’re too big.”

Hjumpik jumps first. He fumbles. Toad Lee tries to catch him and trips also. Bowser, still above, mutters, “Uh oh.”

Ghostly voices echo: “Tonight’s performance: Just Desserts!”

A swarm of spectral actors materializes.

Bowser drops in like a meteor. The battle begins.

The Ghost Battle

Toad Lee tries to charge. Bowser physically restrains him: “No. Only I smash toads.”

He uppercuts the ghost with delight, regaining strength from the hit. When the ghost punches back, its fist passes harmlessly through him.

Hjumpik pulls out a rope; Toad Lee glances through a shattered window and sees flickering lights far in the greenhouse — something terrible is happening outside.

Hjumpik aids Bowser: “If you want to fly, I’ll bring you down!”

He hurls a handaxe. It connects. The Poltergeist shrieks, then releases a telekinetic blast that flings Bowser through a wall into the floor below.

Dan is trying to pull Toadburt up. He coughs out a weak “help.”

The Poltergeist taunts: “Humpy!”

Hjumpik squares up: “Pick on someone your own size. You’ve picked on enough already.”

The ghost’s telekinetic pulse throws Toadburt unconscious. Hjumpik barely keeps footing. Toad Lee strains to hold his axe.

The ghost declares its intent: kill, devour, end the living.

Hjumpik offers a ration. The ghost slaps it away.

He lunges, hammer swinging — time slows — the ghost catches the hammer… but Hjumpik headbutts it instead.

Toad Lee hurls javelins, screaming. The ghost laughs: “I’ll HELP you with that charge!”

Bowser, crawling back in through debris, drops Toadburt gently. “I don’t know your names… but I think you’re Dan,” he mutters, then goes to smash the ghost again.

Dan says, “Good hit, Hjumpik.” Hjumpik, mid-adrenaline, accidentally agrees with himself, “Good job, Hjumpik.”

The Balcony and the Oracle Again

They reach the cracked balcony. The marble tiles sag dangerously over the Foyer below.

In the far distance, Hjumpik spots the Oracle again — nobody else sees him — whispering to ghosts in a language of cold, drifting syllables.

Bowser asks him why he’s staring. Then declares Dan is cannon fodder and should lead. Dan refuses. Trembling.

Bowser grabs Hjumpik’s hand and jumps. The toads tumble off after them.

Archie’s Return and the Ticket

Elsewhere, Archie has just escaped his nightmare and appears in the foyer. A ghost floats before him:

“I am Perrius Annmatar. Tonight’s feature: special guest Smoken Al’.”

It hands him a ticket. Archie stammers, “What does that mean—” The ghost vanishes.

A crash — Bowser, Hjumpik, Dan, Toad Lee, and Toadburt tumble in from above.

Archie and Dan embrace. Hjumpik calls Archie “the three-eyed bandit” again.

The Choice

The Oracle reappears and makes an offer:

He will heal Toad Eager… If the group purges the three demons of the Manor:

The Spider
The Mirror Terror
The Arcane Wrath

The Oracle says they may choose: Fight the Mirror Monster now… Or aid their friends fighting the spiders outside.

They choose their friends.

The party marches together toward the courtyard, ready to face whatever horror has begun the “lightshow” in the greenhouse.
