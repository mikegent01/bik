# Beat list — The Belly of the Beast (Scorncrow, round two → the Skittering Grove)

Session continues straight out of `the_scorncrow_skirmish`, same night.

1. Crows swarm Feyward Dan, pecking; he drops unconscious at Archie's feet. *(existing canon: dan_the_toad)*
2. Archie calls Salam over; Salam is told to stabilise Dan — "keep that thing distracted, it's kinda scary."
3. Salam: "your service won't go in vain." He runs to Dan.
4. Archie announces he is doing "something really risky" and charges the Scorncrow.
5. Archie plants his staff in the ground; the earth tremors. The Scorncrow laughs ("oh, you little thing") and crows lift it off the shaking ground.
6. Scorncrow: "care for a swing?" — a pointed, spiky tree branch comes down.
7. Archie jumps, slams into the Scorncrow knowing the fall will land on Remi. He does it anyway.
8. Scorncrow stumbles, falls, lands on top of Remi. Archie: "I'll make sure it's worth it."
9. Shadow servants surround Wario's motorbike; he is still wrenching it out of the dirt.
10. Eager awakens the Shadow Blade and slices the downed Scorncrow, nearly hitting Remi; backs off fast.
11. Remi, pinned: "well, I have this monster on top of me, I'd better get him off." Archie: "preferably push him onto the tremor I made." Remi: "it's too heavy."
12. Remi realises she is at point-blank range, fires the musket into the Scorncrow's face. The face comes off. **Human blood.** *(new canon fact — the Scorncrow's "I am not a monster" from the last session is corroborated)*
13. Wario destroys two shadow servants; they burst to dust. "You shadow chumps have nothing on Wario."
14. Markop swings; the remaining servants go down. Only the Scorncrow is left.
15. Wario frees the motorbike at last and comes barrelling at Remi/Scorncrow/Archie.
16. The Scorncrow laughs and jumps into the tree's mouth. *(new canon: the tree has a mouth)*
17. Archie: "thanks for the ride." Argues they cannot win this and Dan must be taken to safety. Asks where Green T is — "he seems too powerful."
18. Wario: can't let it get away; that toad over there is poisoned, he's purple — Dan needs sending away.
19. Wario: "we should go to the belly of the beast and uproot it." Remi: if the tree needs to die, kill it from the inside. Archie is compelled to go inside and set it on fire.
20. Green T is already gone at the reunion. Archie suggests celebrating; Wario says Green T has his own agenda, maybe helping another way.
21. Remi honks the motorbike horn. **WAHAHAHAH.** Archie: too loud — a 50-mile radius heard that. *(consequence: the grove knows they are coming)*
22. Feyward Dan heals himself, patches up, spends all his magic: "IT'S OKAY ARCHIE I AM HEALED... no need to worry."
23. Dan mocks Wario: this will be a tough fight, we should all be in this together, you can't handle it alone — and that wasn't half the Scorncrow's power.
24. Wario revs and rides straight into the tree's mouth: "no one challenges Wario to a fight and gets away from it."
25. Archie calls for Remi and Wario. No response. He blames the tree.
26. Remi and Wario are both in cobwebs, inside. The motorbike is gone, unseen.
27. A spider approaches Remi; she runs, nearly bitten, trips into a web.
28. Archie shouts "REMI, WARIO, WHERE ARE YOU" and casts a fire bolt at the tree. Bark damaged. Nothing else happens. Fires again.
29. Fire magic is weakened here; Archie pours ALL of his magic into one cast. His eyes turn to fire. The tree **explodes** — and the exit is sealed. *(consequence: entrance destroyed, party split)*
30. Markop: "what have you done, you've destroyed our only entrance."
31. Archie thinks of moving the bark or enlarging the tree. Markop removes a piece of bark; the jaws of the tree SHUT. Wario (inside) runs.
32. **Combat, inside.** Scorncrow multiattacks Wario's motorbike with Murder of Crows (16 to hit, disadvantage, 14 damage), then the bike again; then Wario twice (17, 13). Shadow Jump as a bonus action.
33. Ettercap (morale BOLD 84) multiattacks, dashes at Remi. Giant Green Spider webs the Guard (DC 12 Str). A second spider rushes the Guard and webs it. Ettercap dashes at Wario. Ettercap casts Web Strand at the Guard (DC 12 Dex) and Reels. Ettercap webs Remi twice. Ettercap dashes at the Guard.
34. Wario retreats from Remi (morale). The Guard spears the Ettercap (13 to hit, 5 damage).
35. Outside: Markop pries the jaws open, sees darkness, then a giant pit below. "Anyone have a rope?" Dan: "I don't think soooo."
36. They throw a torch in as a test. Remi, inside, hears voices: *"there's a fire, someone's causing a fire, they're going to burn us all down"* — and the torch pops back out of the trunk. *(new canon: there are people in there, and they can talk)*
37. Marching order agreed and reasoned: Feyward Dan (paladin, front), Eager, Archie (squishy, middle), Salam, Markop (rear).
38. They descend on Markop's rope into the belly of the beast.
39. Markop recognises where they are: **the Skittering Grove**. The great dead tree, webs for foliage, wrapped beast and man hanging like macabre fruit; dark roots in grey earth. *(location: new entry needed)*
40. Remi fires a bow, misses a spider. Spiders are still everywhere.
41. Remi: "it seems like I hear someone yelling." Markop yells "WHERE ARE YOU!"
42. Archie thinks about spells. Markop finds Wario's motorbike but cannot ride it — he is a centaur.
43. Archie: "if there is a will there is a way."
44. Spiders everywhere — and Legion soldiers also. *(new canon: Iron Legion presence inside the grove)*
45. Remi: the only thing she needs to figure out is how not to die.

- **Location:** the Skittering Grove — the interior of the Scorncrow's tree, reached by rope through the jaws.
- **Characters:** the Scorncrow (human blood under the face); the Ettercap; the Giant Green Spiders; the webbed Guard (unnamed — naming gate: leave unnamed).
- **Facts:** the tree has a mouth and a pit; the exit can be sealed; fire magic is weakened inside; Legion soldiers are inside the grove.
- **Exhibits:** the marching order slip; the torch test; the Scorncrow's face (human blood) — a coroner-style examination note.
