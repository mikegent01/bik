## Slot 1 — LEAD — the mound eating the library

**Source lines:**
> "a wet green haystack the size of a cart, standing in the Overgrown Library
> among the bare joists and the coiling roots, and what it was eating was
> *books*."
> "It took them off the low racks one at a time, unhurried, the way a cow works
> a hedge, and the sound they made going in was the sound of a boot in deep mud."
> "a page came out of the side of its mouth and hung there, browning."
> Location record: "The original flooring has rotted away, leaving only bare
> joists and foundation stonework supported by massive, coiling tree roots."
> "the head librarian: a massive animated tree with golden-red autumn leaves...
> Books hang from his branches like fruit."

**Must appear:** shambling mound the size of a cart, wet matted green;
a book crushed in its maw with **one page protruding and browning**; bare floor
joists with dark drops between them; massive coiling tree roots holding the
structure; low bookracks; at the far west end the rooted tree librarian with
golden-red autumn foliage and books hanging from branches like fruit.
**Cast:** NONE in the foreground — this is the establishing plate of the room
and the threat. Party arrives in slot 2.
**Must NOT appear:** intact modern floorboards, electric light, any lettering
on book spines, people.
**Text in image:** NONE.

**Prompt:**
> Moody painterly digital concept art, dark fantasy archive plate, wide
> cinematic 16:9. The interior of an overgrown fey library seen from a doorway:
> the floor is gone, leaving only bare timber joists with black drops between
> them, the whole structure held up by massive coiling tree roots that have
> burst through the stonework. In the middle distance, standing on the joists, a
> shambling mound the size of a hay cart — a sodden matted mass of green and
> brown vegetation, dripping — is eating a book, one torn page protruding from
> the side of its maw and browning at the edge. Low bookracks around it, half
> emptied, volumes scattered on the joists. At the far end, rooted into the
> floor and wall, an enormous animated tree with a humanoid face and golden-red
> autumn leaves, books hanging from its branches like fruit, motionless and
> watching. Warm amber lantern light from glass orbs on the walls against cold
> green gloom. Palette: mossy green, wet brown, amber lamplight, deep shadow. No
> people, no text, no lettering, no book titles, no watermarks. Oil-painting
> texture, atmospheric depth, high detail.

## Slot 3 — section 7 "The Hallways Are in Surprisingly Good Shape"

**Source lines (supplied verbatim by the GM — the thesis of the section):**
> "The hallways of the fey manor are in surprisingly good shape overall, with
> rather minimal wear or damage. The plaster is cracked in places. The wood has
> seen better days. Everything is clean, and polished as well as it can be."
> "A vast carpet runs in all directions, well worn from the footsteps of
> countless beings, but clean — and it rests upon polished marble that is
> equally worn and equally well cared for."
> "Warm light fills the hallways from numerous glowing glass orb lanterns
> affixed to the walls."
> Waluigi's reading: "somebody is still polishing the marble... the lanterns are
> *warm*."

**Must appear:** long hallway receding; cracked plaster; tired but polished
wood; a vast carpet worn threadbare down its centre line yet clean; polished
marble visible at the carpet's edges, worn but cared for; a row of glowing
glass orb lanterns on the walls; **one or two thin vine tendrils just entering
at the very edge** — the war arriving at a house that is still being maintained.
**Cast:** NONE — place plate. This is the one slot where the empty room *is*
the argument: the point is that nobody is here and it is still spotless.
**Must NOT appear:** people, damage or debris on the carpet, electric light,
text.
**Text in image:** NONE.

**Prompt:**
> Moody painterly digital concept art, dark fantasy archive plate, wide
> cinematic 16:9. A long empty hallway in a fey manor, seen straight down its
> length, receding into warm gloom. The plaster is cracked in places and the
> dark wood panelling has seen better days, but everything is scrupulously clean

> threadbare along its centre line by the footsteps of countless beings yet

> glass orb lanterns are affixed to the walls at intervals, filling the space

> miss. Palette: warm amber, deep brown, cream marble, faint green intrusion. No

> texture, atmospheric depth, high detail.
