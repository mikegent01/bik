# Beat list — the library reclamation session

Source: user transcript, session following `feyward_battalion_of_six_and_the_bait_plan`.
Marks: **NEW** = new canon to file first, **EXISTING** = already in the archive.

1. Hjumpik re-reads Waluipedia articles. Sees Waluigi's line about him — "brains
   and bronze". Waluigi is offended someone is reading. **EXISTING** (Waluipedia,
   Hjumpik, Waluigi)
2. Hjumpik looks left. A guy is eating something. It resolves into a plant
   monster — a shambling mound. **EXISTING** (shambling mounds)
3. The veteran fairy flies up, sees the monster **eating books**. **EXISTING**
   (bait fairy from prior session — user confirmed same character)
4. "Not my library," Hjumpik says. **EXISTING** (`overgrown_library`, has a
   sentient tree head librarian)
5. Hjumpik cannot tell how hurt the big book-eater is. Decides to reclaim the
   library.
6. "We need strategy — as you said in your Waluipedia, I love strategy."
7. The fairy comes over: "we can take him." They go **against** their standing
   plan (up to the main room / the prince).
8. Waluigi is happy to be read. "At least your reading my articles, someone has
   a brain at least."
9. Hjumpik: "I don't know much technology but I like reading." Waluigi: "you
   better get used to it, if we're doing things the right way, you're doing it
   my way."
10. Hjumpik decides marching order: veteran fairy leads (he can lead — "I fought
    wars in Barckelhaven and not one person died"), then Hjumpik, then Toad Lee.
    **NEW** — Barckelhaven is a new place name.
11. Stealth + sneak attack agreed.
12. "Tree guys." Toad Lee: "three guys?" "No, TREE guys." "Oh, okay."
13. Waluigi cuts in front of Toad Lee — "stay with your audience little toad,
    let me show you how a REAL show happens." Toad Lee ends up third, moves to
    Hjumpik's side. They nod.
14. Hjumpik whispers to Waluigi: on three.
15. He swings and misses. Then hits. "How do you miss a distracted stationary
    target?"
16. Goblin staff run up and stab it. Waluigi lands a magic missile, it explodes.
    The shambling mound falls — **it was already weakened; this was overkill.**
17. Toad Lee picks at the corpse. Hjumpik pulls a **yew leaf** (spell component)
    and takes its pointy stick — now a sword. **NEW** — item.
18. Hjumpik pats his man on the back: "that was not that hard right." Toad Lee:
    "sorry, what was that?" — spaced out, not paying attention to the battle.
    **FLAG: third instance of Toad Lee's missing time.**
19. "If we stealth like this we can do anything." Hjumpik flips his warhammer;
    it falls and almost hits his foot. "oh.... sorry about that."
20. Toad Lee says he wants more action. "I know, you're the action toad." "I
    still want to be safe with Hjumpik."
21. They head back to the planning room. Open door → room in open battle: two
    treants and shambling mounds, chaos.
22. Toad Lee: if we fight these two shambling mounds we gain renown and favor in
    the manor — maybe they can help you get back to Archie. **EXISTING** (Archie)
23. Waluigi offers Toad Lee: forget Archie, join his newspaper club, write
    stories for him. Hjumpik: "no thanks."
24. Hjumpik: best bet is to gang up on one of the plant monsters.
25. Hjumpik goes around. Waluigi stops and asks Toad Lee: "you going to follow
    him like a lost dog?" Toad Lee: "I'm not a pet, I just think he is
    reliable." Waluigi is annoyed.
26. They head up to the door.
27. **Hallway description (verbatim from GM):** surprisingly good shape, minimal
    wear; plaster cracked in places, wood seen better days, everything clean and
    polished as well as it can be. Vast carpet running in all directions, well
    worn from countless footsteps, clean, resting on polished marble equally
    worn but well cared for. Warm light from numerous glowing glass orb lanterns
    affixed to the walls. **Use this directly.**
28. Two guards shut a door. They say the creation in there **ate someone**.
    Hjumpik asks to go around and flank. "wait — you saw someone eat someone?"
29. A battle is going on in the kitchen. A commander, **Red** — he, she — is
    preparing the dinner. "She will eat you alive like the monsters. She's the
    heir's greatest soldier." **NEW CHARACTER.**
30. "We're both going to die on our own terms" — Hjumpik: "that's not the
    Hjumpik way." "huh, what's that?" "My name."
31. His friend freaks out the guard. He opens a door to the **mushroom room**;
    they get drunk. **NEW** location detail (mushroom room exists as "mushroom
    coat room door" in prior filing).
32. "Going up can be dangerous." "That's my middle name — Hjumpik Danger
    Deltor." **USER RULING: played as an obvious bluff, canon surname Deldkur.**
33. They leave upstairs.
34. "Ow ow ow," Red says. Who are you? Did orange guy send you?
35. Hjumpik: "you're here to stop the plant monsters?" Red: "no no, we're here
    to cook. The plants love this."
36. The plant she shows **withers in her hand**. Hjumpik gets it: this food is
    bad. **She is poisoning the plants through the kitchen.**
37. She hands Hjumpik a pot of the slop. "You will stay for dessert."
38. Something is **wriggling in an oven**.
39. "Maybe later." "...wait, what's in that door?" Waluigi asks. She gets a key:
    "oh, nothing much — a shortcut to the main room." **NEW** — shortcut door.
40. Guard says the mushroom room didn't have any effect on him.
41. Other guard is missing. "Where did he go?" "He is in the mushroom room, want
    to see?"
42. Hjumpik notices the guard's eyes are **bloodshot** — there IS an effect.
43. In the mushroom room he picks up a mushroom, sniffs it — it's fine.
44. Hjumpik walks away, turns around, to the **bathroom**.
45. A guard is there saying he is guarding **the lady of the manor**.
46. Toad Lee: "you have been here for 15 minutes." "What? She said 20 minutes
    ago." "What did the lady look like?" "She was hiding her face."
47. Open the door. A knock, then open. **A plant monster pops out.**
48. Waluigi throws a fireball — like a spark of fire — hits it in the heart. It
    turns to ash.
49. They investigate the room. Waluigi finds something in the toilet. "Is it
    poop?" Hjumpik asks. **A violet fungus.** **NEW** — item/creature.
50. Hjumpik asks the fungus: "do you speak English?" Then to Waluigi: "can you
    translate this?" "Huh — I'm a field archivist, not a translator." "Maybe
    Waluipedia has it" — starts looking at his tablet.
51. "I'll be right back." Hjumpik heads to the guards — the mushroom guard.
52. A secret hatch opens; the other guard returns.
53. "Hey, the mushrooms didn't have any effects," the other guard said. Mushroom
    guard: "oh, I didn't think you would come back that soon." "Where were you?"
    "Field observations."
54. "Are we winning or losing?" "Ehh...." he looks.
55. "Why should we trust you?" Three reasons: (1) Aurelian helping, (2) Orangus
    Cornelius, (3) "I stopped Wario — actually I did quite a bit."
    **EXISTING** — all three are known canon names.
56. Fine. He knocks three times, a secret door opens. Hjumpik tried this first
    with instructions and failed.
57. A secret passage, one by one, wall — they scurry through. Other people are
    waiting. **They all end up back where they started.**
58. Waluigi is fed up. The confrontation: "we have been just going in circles
    and we're losing. How many people have we lost due to your incompetence —
    and that toad too, he just sits around and follows you. At least the fey
    here CARE about the house."
59. "We killed one of the big plant monsters, right?" "A half-dead one that was
    almost killed by the fey residents."
60. "Do you want to just run around and fight things, or think before you act?"
61. "Well, thinking led us so far. We're not dead. But if we don't act they will
    reach the heir and we will be stuck with the plants forever."
62. "No no no, we can't fight every battle. Look at these guys."
63. "I one-shot that shrub almost 10 minutes ago."
64. "Remember, the goblins stabbed it and it died. I don't think that's saying
    too much."
65. "Then if they're that weak, we should take them on."
66. He opens the door. **The monster stands there surrounded by people. It spots
    them and roars.**
67. "Since when did you care?" Hjumpik says.
68. **"I can't afford not to care anymore," Waluigi says.** ← closing line.
